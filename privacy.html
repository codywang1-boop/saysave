SaySave Privacy Policy

App: SaySave Doc/Image Scanner (Android app app.saysave.scanner and the web version at codywang1-boop.github.io/saysave)
Last updated: November 16, 2026

SaySave helps you scan documents and save files into folders in your own Google Drive. We built it so your documents stay between your phone and your Google Drive. SaySave has no server of its own and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What SaySave accesses and why

  • Your Google Drive (with your permission). When you tap Allow, Google gives SaySave permission to see your Drive folder names and file names, upload files, create folders, and move files. SaySave uses this only to: suggest the right folder for a file, save the file there, create a folder when you approve it, and (only if you use the optional Organize feature) move files you review and approve. SaySave never deletes files, never changes sharing settings, and never reads the contents of files already in your Drive.
  • Files you choose, share, or scan. The file is sent directly from your phone to your Google Drive. To pick a folder and a name, SaySave reads the file name and, for scans or unclear names, the text on the first pages. This reading happens on your device.
  • Camera. Used only when you tap Scan or take a photo or video to save. Scanning uses Google's on-device document scanner.
  • Microphone. Used only while the microphone button is active, to understand voice commands such as "Save to my Google Drive". Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (for most Android phones, Google's).

What is stored

  • On your device only: your settings, voice commands, folder shortcuts, learned folder rules, a short list of recent saves (for Undo), and your Google account email (so you don't have to pick it each time).
  • In your Google Drive: the files and folders you choose to save there.
  • SaySave does not store your files or data on any other server. There are no ads and no analytics.

Third parties

SaySave talks only to Google services needed for the features above: Google Drive API (saving files), Google sign-in, Google's on-device document scanner, and your phone's speech recognition. To read text on scanned pages, the app may download an open-source text-recognition library (Tesseract.js) from the jsDelivr content network the first time; your images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SaySave's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never transferred to others, never used for advertising, and never read by humans.

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time in SaySave → Settings → Disconnect, or remove SaySave's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.
  • Children: SaySave is not directed at children under 13.
