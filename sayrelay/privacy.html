SayRelay Privacy Policy

App: SayRelay: Voice Translator (Android app com.sayrelay.keyboard)
Last updated: November 23, 2026

SayRelay is a voice keyboard. You speak, and it types a translation of what you said into the text field you are using. SayRelay has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What SayRelay accesses and why

  • Microphone. Used only after you tap Speak on the SayRelay keyboard, and only until you tap Done or Cancel or stop talking. The microphone never turns on by itself.
  • Speech recognition. Your speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service installed on your phone (on most Android phones, Google's). That service may process your audio on its own servers, under its own privacy policy. SayRelay receives only the resulting text.
  • Translation. The text is translated on your phone using Google ML Kit. The first time you use a language pair, the app downloads that language model from Google. After that, translation happens on the device.
  • The text field you are typing in. SayRelay inserts the translated text into the field you chose. It does not read, store, or send the rest of what is in that field. Voice input is turned off in password fields.

What is stored

  • On your device only: your chosen spoken language and output language, and the downloaded translation models.
  • SayRelay does not keep a history of what you said or typed, and it never sends messages for you.

Third parties

SayRelay relies on two Google components: your phone's speech recognition service and Google ML Kit Translation. Google ML Kit may contact Google servers to download language models and may send Google basic, non-personal information about the device and app for diagnostics and performance, as described in Google's ML Kit terms. SayRelay itself sends no data anywhere.

Your choices

  • You can turn off microphone access in Android Settings at any time, or switch to another keyboard.
  • Uninstalling the app removes your settings and the downloaded language models.
  • SayRelay is not directed at children under 13.
